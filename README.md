# Greenhouse fixing hardware glossary

Plain-language definitions of the parts that hold a polyhouse film and frame together. Use it to read a quotation or write a bill of materials.

- **Wire clamp / film clamp:** a spring or plastic clamp that grips the film against a pipe or profile. Check pipe diameter and film thickness it is rated for.
- **Zig zag spring (wiggle wire):** a formed spring wire that locks film into an aluminium or galvanised channel. Specify the wire gauge and coating.
- **Film channel (lock profile):** the rail the spring sits in. It must match the spring and the frame fixing.
- **Turn buckle:** tensions wire in trellis and orchard systems. Ask for load rating and thread size.
- **Hook and eye fixings:** carry cable or wire from posts or the structure.
- **Twine and plant clips:** support crops on trellis lines inside the house.
- **Hole punch tool:** makes clean holes for drip and fixing points.

Quote lines should state item, size, coating, quantity and packing so each can be counted on arrival.
